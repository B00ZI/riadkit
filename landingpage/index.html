The guest journey

Small changes, a calmer stay

Every riad runs its own way. Here's what changes when guest information and requests live in one place.

Without a centralized guest portal

  • Guests may need to ask staff for basic information.
  • Service information can be scattered across paper and messages.
  • Requests may reach the team through several different channels.
  • Available services can be harder for guests to discover.

With RiadKit

  • Essential information is accessible through a QR code.
  • Services and recommendations are presented in one place.
  • Requests appear in an organized dashboard.
  • Guests can explore the riad's offerings more easily.
